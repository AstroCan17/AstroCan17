The IPF prototype connects a synthetic raw-data producer (Sentinel-2 MSI Synthetic Raw Data Generator), a multispectral consumer (msi-processor), and a shared data-store registry. The generator runs S2B L1B backwards through the operational radiometric chain, materialises Synthetic L0 products and a calibration database, and the processor consumes those inputs for a non-tautological L0→L1B round-trip.
